💡 How Manual Paybill Works

  • Customers pay into your Co-op Paybill from M-Pesa or Bank App.
  • Customer enters the SMS confirmation code and payer name at checkout.
  • Order enters the cashier's "Pending Paybill Verifications" live queue.
  • Cashier compares the code and amount against the shop's mobile SMS alert.
  • With one tap, cashier confirms payment and marks order as PAID.

Audit Trail
